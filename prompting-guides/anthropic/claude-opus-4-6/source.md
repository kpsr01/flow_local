# Claude Opus 4.6 prompting guidance (official excerpt)

Source: https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/build-with-claude/prompt-engineering/claude-prompting-best-practices

Source date: 2026-09-11 (retrieved for this policy)
Model family: Claude Opus 4.6

Relevant official guidance retained here:

- Opus 4.6 can do more upfront exploration at higher effort; replace blanket defaults with targeted instructions and remove over-prompting.
- If thinking is excessive, constrain reasoning or lower effort.
- Clear, direct instructions and specific output format/constraints improve results.
- Use sequential steps or bullets when order or completeness matters.
- Opus 4.6 may overengineer; keep solutions simple and focused when that is the desired behavior.
