# Claude Opus 4.7 prompting guidance (official excerpt)

Source: https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/build-with-claude/prompt-engineering/claude-prompting-best-practices

Source date: 2026-09-11 (retrieved for this policy)
Model family: Claude Opus 4.7

Relevant official guidance retained here:

- Be clear and direct, with specific output format and constraints.
- Use sequential steps or bullets when order or completeness matters.
- At low effort, use explicit checklists for tasks with multiple sections.
- At higher effort, use the model's reasoning rather than prescribing a detailed procedure.
